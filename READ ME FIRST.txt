IMAGE STUDIO v2.0
=================

FIRST TIME ONLY
  1. Double-click "Install Image Studio" (already done on this PC).

EVERY TIME
  2. Double-click "Start Image Studio".
     Your browser opens to the app. Keep the black window open while
     you use it; close that window to shut the app down.
     After an update, the first start installs new parts (needs internet once).

SCREENS
  Create   Type an idea, pick a style/shape, Generate. Pick one of your
           trained models in the Model menu to put your character in a scene.
  Gallery  Everything you've made. Click a picture for details. "Select" lets
           you delete several at once. Deleted pictures go to the Recycle Bin.
  Train    Teach the app your own character: new project > add 15-40 pictures
           (or split a reference sheet) > Train. Takes about 1-3 hours.

FOLDERS
  My Images   your pictures (each stores the prompt that made it)
  Training    your training projects and their pictures
  My Models   your finished trained models

PRIVATE
  Runs only on this PC. Making pictures never uses the internet.
  (Training downloads a small add-on once, the first time you train.)

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  Send Claude a screenshot of the black window, or studio_log.txt
  (or train_log.txt inside a project folder for training problems).

ENGINE
  FLUX.1 Schnell by Black Forest Labs, Apache 2.0 license. Training add-on
  ostris/FLUX.1-schnell-training-adapter, Apache 2.0.
